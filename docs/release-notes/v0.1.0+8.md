- feature: On wide phones and foldables, and on short landscape windows, navigation moves to a side rail; Settings > Appearance puts it on the left or the right.
- feature: Large system text sizes follow Apple's Dynamic Type sizes and pages reflow instead of clipping; tab bars stay compact and show a large preview on long press.
- feature: Settings > Storage shows the total and what it is made of, and Clear cache also clears images in memory, video frames and temporary files.
- improvement: The app icon badge, the notification shade and Activity show the same unread count; each conversation has one notification, removed once you read it anywhere.
- improvement: Opening the app from a notification shows the new message in Activity right away.
- improvement: Attachments not opened for 30 days and leftovers of signed-out accounts are removed from the cache; video posters and list previews reuse what is already on disk.
- improvement: Profile pictures are downloaded again only when they change.
- improvement: Images and video frames use less memory and are released when the app goes to the background.
- fix: Android Settings > About shows the update card, the build number and the release notes again.
- fix: The phone image viewer keeps tall and zoomed images clear of the bars, a single tap hides the bottom caption and strip, and a playing video's controls can be shown again.
- fix: Pinch and double-tap work while the original image is still loading, and an expanded caption shows all its lines.
- fix: Opening a conversation from Activity or a notification no longer misses messages that arrived while it was closed.
- fix: Plan & Billing on phones reloads after the plan changes.
