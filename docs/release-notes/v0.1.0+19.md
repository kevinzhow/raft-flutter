- improvement: Forms (new channel, new task, invite, profile, create agent, report an issue, sidebar sections, workspace and server settings, and more) open as a bottom sheet on phones and narrow windows and as a dialog on wider ones. The title and actions stay in place while the fields scroll, and the field you are typing in stays above the keyboard. Resizing the window switches between the two without closing the form or losing what you typed.
- improvement: A form with unsaved changes asks before it is discarded, whether you drag it down, tap outside it, press Esc or Back, or tap ×. Cancel closes it without asking.
- fix: Bottom sheets can be dragged by their handle and header with a mouse, on a desktop window narrowed to phone width.
